4. Shariah Compliance Framework

A screening framework helps investors avoid prohibited sectors and filter out financially non-compliant companies.

Learn more on Shariah Screening
  • Review business activity and avoid prohibited industries.
  • Check debt and interest-related financial ratios.
  • Use tools like Islamicly and Zoya for initial screening.
  • Apply manual review for edge cases and doubtful names.

5. Risks

Understanding the main risks helps users invest with better discipline and realistic return expectations.

Learn more on Investment Risks
  • Market volatility, especially in mid-cap and small-cap stocks.
  • Currency risk from INR movement against AED or USD.
  • Regulatory changes affecting foreign investor access.
  • Liquidity risk in smaller or less-traded securities.

6. Suggested Investment Approach

Users should be guided toward products based on experience level, time commitment, and risk tolerance.

Learn more on Portfolio Strategy

Beginner

Start with ethical mutual funds and simple screened products that require less monitoring.

Advanced
